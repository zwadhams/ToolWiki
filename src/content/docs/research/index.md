My research examines how static analysis tools are used to assess software security and quality. In these notes, I summarize work with my collaborators and discuss what our findings mean for developers using these tools.

## Topics

- **[Comparing SAST tools](./comparing-tools/).** An empirical study of detection patterns across twelve Open Charge Point Protocol (OCPP) implementations, with attention to language-specific analysis capabilities.
- **[SAST adoption](./sast-adoption/).** A literature review of usability problems that affect adoption and continued use, including false positives, poor output, setup effort, and workflow disruption.
- **[SAST in CI/CD](./sast-workflow/).** A process for transferring SAST findings into existing issue-tracking software, demonstrated with SonarQube and GitLab.
- **[Software quality models](./software-quality/).** A proposed application of hierarchical software quality assurance (HSQA) to electric vehicle charging software, connecting analysis results to quality and security characteristics.
- **[Malware sophistication](./binary-analysis/).** A proposed approach to malware quality assessment using characteristics that can be investigated through static binary analysis.

## Papers

I cover a literature review, a CI/CD use case, an empirical comparison, and two proposed applications of software quality assessment. I discuss recommendations separately from our reported results.

My [master's thesis, On Improving the Adoption, Usability, and Retention of Static Application Security Testing (SAST) Tools](https://www.cs.montana.edu/izurieta/thesis/Wadhams.pdf) brings together the adoption review and CI/CD work. Its chapters and the corresponding conference papers describe the same research.
